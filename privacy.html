Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version

  • Wesoxch has no adverts, and we never sell your data.
  • We do not use tools that follow you across apps or websites.
  • Wesoxch does not record events. It only helps people attend.
  • You can delete your account, and everything you added, from inside the app.

Who we are

Wesoxch is a community app for people in East Africa, made in Kisumu, Kenya. In this policy, "we" means the Wesoxch team. You can reach us at wesoxchkenya@gmail.com.

What we do not do

  • We do not show adverts or use an advertising ID.
  • We do not use analytics tools that build a profile of you.
  • We do not read your contacts.
  • We do not record who looks at your profile.
  • We do not use your camera or microphone. We only receive the photos you choose to upload.
  • We do not take payments in the app yet.

Who we share data with

We never sell your data. A few services help us run Wesoxch, and each one only gets what it needs:

  • Africa's Talking sends your one-time code by SMS, so it receives your phone number.
  • Our hosting provider stores the data and runs the server.
  • Expo and Google deliver notifications to your phone.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or if it is needed to keep someone safe.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you added stay until you delete your account.
  • One-time codes are removed within about a day.
  • Our hosting provider keeps server logs for a short time.
  • If we keep backups, deleted data can remain in a backup for up to 30 days. It is not used, and it is then overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your name, bio, picture and phone details in the app at any time.
  • You can say no to location and still use Wesoxch.
  • You can block people from Settings.
  • You can delete your account in the app. See how to delete your account.
  • You can email us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. We will reply within 30 days.

Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 gives you rights over your personal data. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

Age

Wesoxch is for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.

Our Therapy is peer support

Our Therapy is a place for people to listen to each other. It is not professional care and it is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, please contact your local emergency services.

Security

We use encrypted connections and limit who can reach the database. No service is perfectly secure, so please tell us at once if you think something is wrong.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is important, we will tell you in the app.

Contact

Email wesoxchkenya@gmail.com, or write to us at Wesoxch, Kisumu, Kenya.
