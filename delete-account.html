Delete your Wesoxch account

This page explains how to permanently delete your account on the Wesoxch app, made by Wesoxch in Kisumu, Kenya.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Wesoxch.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

It happens straight away. If you uninstalled the app, install it again and sign in with your phone number (we text you a code), then follow the same steps.

If you cannot use the app

Email wesoxchkenya@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Wesoxch account". Tell us your username and the phone number on the account. We may ask a question only the account owner could answer, to make sure it is you. We will delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and your profile picture.
  • Your listings, rentals and events, and their photos.
  • Your messages in communities and in Our Therapy.
  • Your community memberships and your replies to events.
  • Your blocks, reports you made, and your notification code.
  • Your phone number.

Communities you started

If other people have joined a community you started, it stays open and the member who has been there the longest becomes its admin. The community's picture stays with it. If you are its only member, the community is deleted.

What we keep

Nothing about you is kept once your account is deleted, except in backups, if we keep any. Deleted data can remain in a backup for up to 30 days, is not used, and is then overwritten.
